Guild Guidelines & Rules

1. Server & Guild Rules

Hello! Here are the forbidden things and foundational rules on the <Blackwing> server that we expect all members to follow to keep this quiet, peaceful corner enjoyable for everyone. Welcome to <Blackwing>!

Politics NO Politics
Strictly Forbidden
Political discussions, debates, propaganda, or divisive arguments of any kind are strictly forbidden in guild chat, Discord channels, and voice comms. Keep our corner peaceful, neutral, and focused on the game.
Racism NO Racism in Any Shape or Form
Zero Tolerance
Zero tolerance for racism, racial slurs, discriminatory language, or prejudice in any shape or form. Any violation will result in an immediate strike, disciplinary action, or permanent guild removal without warning.
Conduct Bashing or Harassment of Members
Zero Tolerance
Bashing, bullying, harassment, personal attacks, or toxic call-outs targeting other members of the <Blackwing> community are strictly forbidden. Treat every teammate with common courtesy and respect.
Raiding Know Your Class !!!
Raid Standard
Understand your class specialization, optimal talent builds, rotations, stat priorities, and boss tactic mechanics inside out. Come prepared with full gems, top-tier enchants, and consumables. Continual self-improvement and dedication to your role are expected from every raider.
Commitment Full Raid Duration & Good Manners
Expected Conduct
You are expected to be here for the full duration of the raid. Leaving without telling anyone is not tolerated. If you can't play or need to step out, let us know in advance—have good manners!
Teamwork Don't Expect Anyone to Boost You
Reciprocal Kindness
Don't expect anyone to boost you. Favors will be returned, it depends on you! A little kindness goes a long way.

Review Schedule

The Officer Council systematically reviews the guild roster on scheduled intervals:

  • Promotions Discussion: Evaluated every 2 weeks.
  • Demotions Discussion: Evaluated every 1 month.

Demotion Rules

  • Inactivity: Inactive raiders are demoted 1 rank every month.
  • Discord Policy: Inactive members are not kicked from Discord; their Discord rank is set to Trial.
  • Performance: Consistently underperforming raiders will also be subject to rank demotion.

Guild Kicks & Discord Bans

Players who fail to follow guild rules, exhibit toxic behavior, or disrupt the guild environment are subject to guild kick and Discord ban. All situations may be discussed within the Officer Council, but the final decision always rests with the Guild Master.

3. Guild DKP & Loot Rules

Official Guidelines for 25-Man ICC & Ruby Sanctum Raids
Open DKP Ladder Table

Blackwing operates a transparent, account-wide DKP bidding economy across all 25-man Heroic raids and high-value trinkets. Read the official guidelines below regarding bidding mechanics, boss point yields, penalties, and the 10,000 DKP cap.

3.1 Core Rules & Updates

Account-Wide Shared DKP Pool Across All Characters
?main <MainName>
All mains and alts share a single unified DKP pool. Inform a Raid Leader before raiding on an alt for the first time. Link your alt to your main anytime in Discord using the command: ?main <MainCharacterName>.
Bonuses Checking Balance & Starter Grants
+50 DKP Starter • +50 DKP Tank/Healer
  • Checking DKP: Check your current balance anytime via your in-game Officer Note or on Discord by typing ?dkp.
  • Welcome Bonus: New raiders receive an automatic starter grant of +50 DKP upon joining.
  • Role Bonus: Dedicated Tanks and Healers receive an extra +50 DKP role bonus.
  • Main Spec Changes: Any permanent or temporary Main Spec adjustments must be declared in the designated Discord channel before the raid starts.

3.3 Bidding Rules & Commands

Auction Mechanics & Ties

  • Auction Timer: A 10-second countdown begins once bidding slows down. Any valid higher bid resets the timer.
  • Ties: The first submitted bid holds priority. Matching an existing highest bid will not overtake the lead.
  • Bid Commands:
    X — Bids a specific number (e.g. 150).
    all / max / all in — Bids your entire DKP pool. Bypasses standard increments, but must exceed the current high bid by at least +5 DKP.

3.5 Bench, Standby & Penalties

Benched & Standby Raiders

If requested to sit out on standby, claim +75 DKP immediately, plus guaranteed priority invite for the next raid of the same type.
*Exception: RS Marathon runs grant next-raid priority only (no +75 bench DKP).

Raid Mistakes & Deductions

  • Minor Mistakes: Tolerated once. Repeating the same mistake results in -50 DKP per occurrence.
  • Major Wipe Mistakes: Critical failures causing a full raid wipe incur -100 DKP.
  • Officer Review: Officers determine penalties. Do not argue during raid pulls; disputes are discussed constructively after the raid.

Leaving Early & Disconnects

  • Leaving Early Unannounced: Penalized with -100 DKP. Real life always comes first—simply notify a Raid Leader before leaving to avoid any penalty.
  • Disconnects & Technical Issues: Technical problems are not penalized. Please send a quick message in Discord or phone to keep the raid leader informed.

3.6 Blackwing 10,000 DKP Cap

There is a strict maximum cap of 10,000 DKP per account. Once you reach 10,000 DKP, you will cease earning additional DKP until you spend points on gear or upgrades. Be sure to monitor your balance regularly and invest your points to prevent losing out on raid rewards!
