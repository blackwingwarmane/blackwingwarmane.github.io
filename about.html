About Blackwing

Who We Are

Founded by seasoned Mythic raiders and long-time friends, Blackwing was established to provide an efficient, respectful, and high-performance raiding environment without the toxicity often associated with hardcore progression.

We believe that preparation, mechanical consistency, and clear communication are far more effective than grinding endless hours. We raid 2 nights per week with zero overtime, aiming for Cutting Edge every tier while maintaining healthy real-life balances.

Guild Culture

Zero toxicity, constructive feedback, team-first mindset.

Raid Philosophy

Every pull has a purpose. We study logs, optimize strategies, and respect raiders' time.

Beyond Raiding

Active Discord voice channels, weekly community alt raids, high key pushes, and game nights.
